Before you ask

Frequently asked questions.

Will a student be treating me?
You will be treated by a licensed dentist. AIDM’s orthodontic residents are qualified dentists undertaking advanced postgraduate training, and every plan is reviewed by board-certified faculty who teach the specialty. That two-clinician model is precisely why the fee is what it is.
Why is the braces package $2,950?
$2,950 is a promotional fee for the first 100 eligible orthodontic patients, and it reflects AIDM’s teaching model — residents in advanced postgraduate training treating under board-certified faculty supervision. It applies to patients who pay the treatment fee in full and cannot be combined with insurance benefits, membership discounts, financing promotions or other offers.
What is included in the complimentary evaluation?
A personalized orthodontic evaluation with the team, plus the records needed to plan your case: panoramic imaging, cephalometric imaging and analysis, clinical photographs and a digital 3D intraoral scan, each when indicated. There is no charge and no referral is required.
Can I use insurance or a payment plan instead?
Yes — AIDM accepts many PPO plans, and financing and payment options may be available subject to approval. They just cannot be stacked on top of the $2,950 promotional rate. At your evaluation the team will show you both routes side by side.
How long does treatment take?
Most cases run 12 to 24 months. Minor corrections can be shorter, and complex cases can take longer — you will be given a realistic range at your evaluation rather than an average.
Am I too old for braces?
No. Orthodontics is effective for children, teens and adults alike. Treatment can also be combined with cosmetic, restorative or surgical work, coordinated across specialties in the same building.
Is retention included, or is that extra later?
Standard orthodontic retention is included in the package, as is appliance removal. What is not included is a replacement appliance or retainer — if one is lost or broken, that is charged separately.
Do I need a referral, and where do I park?
No referral is needed. There is a garage immediately next door at 1401 Philomena Street and parking is free. The building is in Mueller, minutes from I-35 and 51st Street.
