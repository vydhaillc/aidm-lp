Your first visit, step by step.

Nothing is treated on the day you arrive unless you are in pain and ask for it. The first visit exists to find out where you stand and to put a plan and a price in front of you.

  1. 1 Arrive & check in

    Park free in the garage next door. Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications. Forms can be completed online before you come.

  2. 2 X-rays, as needed

    Digital images are taken only where they are clinically indicated — enough to see between and beneath the teeth, and no more than that.

  3. 3 Comprehensive examination

    Teeth, existing work, gums, bite and soft tissues, plus an oral-cancer screening. A cavity-risk assessment tells you which habits are actually driving the problems.

  4. 4 Your plan, in writing

    Findings are shown to you on screen, ranked into what needs doing now and what can wait, with the fee for each step written down before anything is scheduled.

If a professional cleaning is what you also need, it is booked separately or as part of the Establish New Care package — cleaning and fluoride are not part of the $100 special.

Why it matters

What hurts is rarely what started it.

By the time a tooth aches, the decay has usually been under way for months. A comprehensive examination with X-rays finds the things you cannot see or feel yet — decay between teeth, bone loss under the gum line, cracked fillings, an abscess forming at a root tip — while they are still small, cheap and painless to deal with.

  • A cavity caught on an X-ray is a filling; the same cavity found by pain is often a root canal and a crown
  • Gum disease is painless in its early stages and is the leading cause of adult tooth loss
  • Every exam includes an oral-cancer screening of the lips, tongue, cheeks and throat

Who treats you

Two clinicians. One fee.

AIDM is a teaching institute. Residents — already qualified dentists in advanced postgraduate training — treat under the direct supervision of licensed clinical faculty. You get more clinical attention, not less, and the economics of the teaching mission are why a comprehensive new-patient exam and X-rays can be $100.

  • A second, faculty-level opinion built into the plan at no extra charge
  • Orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics, surgery, prosthodontics and paediatrics under one roof
  • Every fee written down before anything is scheduled — no surprise invoices

Before you ask

Frequently asked questions.

What exactly do I get for $100?
A comprehensive dental examination, dental X-rays where they are clinically indicated, a cavity-risk assessment with oral-hygiene guidance, and a personalized treatment plan reviewed with you before you leave. A professional cleaning and fluoride are not included in the $100 — they are priced separately, or bundled into the $350 Establish New Care package.
Why isn’t the cleaning included?
Because we do not yet know which cleaning you need. A routine preventive cleaning and the deeper scaling that gum disease requires are different treatments at different fees, and which one is appropriate is exactly what the examination and X-rays determine. Quoting a cleaning before the exam would mean quoting the wrong one. If you would rather have it all in one visit, Establish New Care at $350 covers the exam, the X-rays and the routine cleaning together.
Will a student be treating me?
You will be treated by a licensed dentist. AIDM is a teaching institute, so residents — already qualified dentists undertaking advanced postgraduate training — treat under the direct supervision of licensed clinical faculty, and your plan is reviewed by faculty before it is quoted to you. That two-clinician model is precisely why the fee is what it is.
Do I need insurance? Can I use mine?
No insurance is needed — the $100 special is a self-pay package and is one of the reasons it exists. If you do have a plan, AIDM accepts many PPOs and will happily bill it, but promotional package pricing cannot be combined with insurance benefits, membership pricing or other discounts. At your visit the team will show you both routes side by side so you can pick whichever leaves you better off.
How long does the first visit take, and what should I bring?
Allow 60 to 90 minutes. Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take. New patient forms can be completed online beforehand, which usually saves fifteen minutes at the desk.
I’m in pain today. Is this the right appointment?
If you are in pain, ask for the emergency appointment instead — AIDM keeps same-day emergency slots, Monday to Saturday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., with an onsite surgical centre for urgent extractions. Call (737) 434-2436 and say it is urgent. A problem-focused emergency evaluation is a different appointment from the comprehensive new-patient exam.
Can I bring my children on the same day?
Yes — AIDM treats children and adults in the same building, and families are routinely booked back to back. Children under three are seen under the First Smile Visit package at $75; older children have their own preventive package. Tell us how many people are coming when you book and we will hold the time.
Do I need a referral, and where do I park?
No referral is needed. There is a garage immediately next door at 1401 Philomena Street and parking is free. The building is in Mueller, minutes from I-35 and 51st Street, and we are open Monday to Saturday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
